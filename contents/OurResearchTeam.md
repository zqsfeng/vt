The Vibrant Team is committed to advancing the understanding and control of structural vibrations, with particular emphasis on wind-induced effects, structural health monitoring, and high-performance vibration mitigation. Led by Prof. Zhouquan Feng, the team is part of a larger research group directed by Prof. Zhengqing Chen (Member of the Chinese Academy of Engineering) and Prof. Xugang Hua.

The team’s research draws on a broad range of interdisciplinary tools and expertise, including structural dynamics, Bayesian inference, intelligent optimization, machine learning, digital twins, smart sensing technologies, and innovative vibration control systems. Their work focuses on understanding and managing the complex dynamic behavior of infrastructure—such as bridges and wind turbine structures—subjected to diverse environmental and operational loads, including wind, waves, temperature variations, traffic, pedestrians, and earthquakes. The goal is to develop efficient, reliable, and durable solutions that enhance structural safety, resilience, and intelligence.

The team welcomes passionate and motivated students and postdoctoral researchers to contribute to its ongoing work at the frontiers of structural vibration and control, and actively seeks collaborations with academic and industry partners worldwide.
